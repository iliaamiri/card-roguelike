    Juniper

    Blackjack and poker, one run at a time.

    How to play

    First time? Play starts a guided opening with fixed cards. After learning the loop and reaching fight 2, later runs are random with optional hints. Replay tutorial is always available from Menu.

    Each run starts at 100 HP and lasts four fights. The last one is a boss. Your HP carries over between fights, and you recover up to 10 HP after each win.

    Your turn

    You have 3 energy and a hand of 5 cards from a shuffled 52-card deck. Choose one full-turn poker play (3 energy), or blackjack rounds (1 energy per Deal). End turn triggers the enemy's displayed attack, then draws a new hand and refills energy.

    Blackjack

    Deal (1 energy) draws 2 cards from the deck, not your poker hand; the enemy gets 2, one face down. Hit to take another card, stand to stop, and the enemy draws until it reaches 17. Closest to 21 without going over wins.

    Win and the enemy takes damage equal to your total (a natural blackjack deals 30). Lose or bust and the enemy hits you immediately, in addition to its End turn attack. A tie refunds your energy.

    Poker

    Select 1 to 5 cards from your hand. The best poker hand among them is played, from high card up to a royal flush. Bigger hands hit harder; pairs give shield and flushes heal. The full list is in the side panel.

    Each play costs 3 energy and discards your entire hand, including unplayed cards. There is no mid-turn redraw: draw your next hand next turn. High card deals 1.5 times the highest card's value, rounded up and capped at 15 damage. A pair grants 25 shield; a flush heals up to 30 HP.

    Shield absorbs attacks before HP and persists across turns, capped at 40. It clears when moving to the next enemy. Select cards with a pointer or Tab and Space.
